Bootstrap job submitted: {{ include "corda-bootstrap.fullname" . }}-bootstrapper

Wait for it to finish before the node releases are ready:

  kubectl wait job/{{ include "corda-bootstrap.fullname" . }}-bootstrapper \
    --for=condition=complete \
    --namespace={{ .Release.Namespace }} \
    --timeout=600s

On success the following resources are created in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}:

  Secrets : corda-notary-certs, corda-node1-certs, corda-node2-certs
            corda-notary-secrets, corda-node1-secrets, corda-node2-secrets
  ConfigMap: corda-network-params

If you need to re-bootstrap (e.g. after network-down), delete the job first:

  kubectl delete job {{ include "corda-bootstrap.fullname" . }}-bootstrapper -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

Then re-run helmfile apply.
